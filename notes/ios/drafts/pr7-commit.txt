feat(ios): add the home screen widgets and move the store into the App Group

- The app and the widget share the App Group group.app.geunal. The core's store moves into its core/ directory once,
  before FreshInstall looks, and opens from the old place when it cannot move; the keychain query stays as it was.
- Small and medium widgets (m1.md screen 14) read the snapshot the app writes: today's events and tomorrow's, the
  empty and start states, and yesterday's tomorrow once midnight passes before the app writes again.
- The app writes the core's widgetSnapshot() when the calendar changes, on coming to the front, after onboarding and
  in the background refresh, only when it changed; a sign-out and a deleted account remove it.
- Tapping a widget opens today in the month view, or its event in the editor from core #55's event ids.
- Debug builds draw the widgets inside the app with -GeunalWidgetGallery for the 14-widgets screenshot.
